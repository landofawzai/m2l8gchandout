# Life with God — Update Spec (v7.4 → v7.5)

**Purpose:** Clarify the relationship between Ministry TO the Lord (M2L) and the Presence of God.
M2L is *drawing near to* God's presence; it is not *the same as* His presence. He is already with us
and in us through Jesus. M2L is turning toward Him, sitting with Him, letting go, and knowing Him.

**Constraints for all edits:**
- Keep the existing voice: short sentences, plain words, no theological jargon.
- Do not remove any existing content unless a replacement is specified below.
- Front page (sections A–D) is space-tight. Edits to A add ~5 lines total; trim spacing rather than content.
- Scripture references stay in the existing format, e.g. `(Mt 22:37-38)`.

---

## 1. Section A — Ministry TO the Lord

### 1a. Add after the first paragraph (after "…just to be with him.")

```
He is already with you, whether or not you feel Him (Gen 28:16; 1 Pet 1:8).
You are not trying to get His attention. You are giving Him yours.
```

### 1b. Replace step 4

**Old:**
```
4. Confess your sins honestly. Confession clears the way to be near Him.
```

**New:**
```
4. Confess your sins honestly. The way is already open through Jesus (Heb 10:19).
   Confession lets you come with a true heart (Heb 10:22).
```

### 1c. Insert a new step after step 5 ("When you catch yourself making requests…")

Renumber the remaining steps (old 6→7, old 7→8).

```
6. Be still. Let go of your striving — that is what "be still" means (Ps 46:10).
   Listen, like Mary at His feet (Lk 10:39). He speaks, He cleanses, He gives.
   Your part is to stay.
```

## 2. Section J — Key Words

### 2a. Add a new key word entry, placed **first**, before "Ministry TO the Lord"

```
The Presence of God — God Himself, with you and in you through Jesus.
Not a feeling. Not a place you must reach. He is already there.
Ministry TO the Lord is turning toward Him.
John 14:23; 1 Corinthians 3:16; Psalm 139:7-10

### 2b. Append one sentence to the "Ministry TO the Lord" entry

**Old (last sentence):**
```
It is like a child who wants to be with her father, just to be with him.
```

**New:**
```
It is like a child who wants to be with her father, just to be with him.
It is not the same as His presence. He is the Presence. Ministry TO the Lord
is coming to sit with Him.

## 3. Back page — "Why Ministry TO the Lord Comes First"

### 3a. Replace the first sentence of "The pattern"

**Old:**
```
At Antioch, the leaders were before the Lord in worship and fasting when the Holy Spirit spoke
and sent Barnabas and Paul to the nations.
```

**New:**
```
At Antioch, the leaders were before the Lord in worship and fasting — letting go of even food
to be with Him. While they were ministering to Him, the Holy Spirit spoke and sent Barnabas
and Paul to the nations.
```

### 3b. Add a short paragraph after "The trap", before "The pattern"

Heading: **The seat**

```
God does not only want us to come. He wants us to sit. Mary sat at His feet and listened.
When we sit, the work shifts to Him: He speaks, He cleanses, He feeds. "Be still" in Psalm 46
means let go — drop your hands. Then the verse finishes: "and know that I am God." Sitting is
not the end. Knowing Him is.
```

### 3c. Add these anchor texts to the existing list (keep existing entries; insert in this order after "Exodus 33:14–15")

```
• Hebrews 10:19–22 — We have confidence to enter by the blood of Jesus. Let us draw near.
• James 4:8 — Draw near to God and He will draw near to you.
• Psalm 46:10 — Be still (let go), and know that I am God.
• Ephesians 2:6 — God seated us with Him.
• John 14:23 — We will come to him and make our home with him.
```

## Summary of what changed and why

| Change | Reason |
|---|---|
| Define "Presence of God" | Title of the diagram was never defined; readers could hear it as a feeling or a place |
| "He is already with you" in A | Section G had this; A did not. The door is already open |
| Confession wording | Heb 10:19–22: Jesus opens the way; confession is coming with a true heart |
| New "Be still" step | All seven steps were things we do; none was receiving. Adds Ps 46:10 (let go) and Lk 10:39 (listen) |
| "The seat" paragraph | Names the shift from coming to sitting, and from sitting to knowing |
| Antioch clause | Makes the simultaneity clear: the Spirit spoke *while* they ministered |
| New anchor texts | Cover "already there," "free to enter," and "let go," which the list lacked |
